## Token authorities

At Genesis:

- **Mint Authority:** revoked after the Genesis supply was created.
- **Freeze Authority:** revoked.
- **Transfer Fee:** none.
- **Transfer Hook:** none.
- **Permanent Delegate:** none.
- **Token extensions:** none.

The Genesis supply is therefore fixed at **100,000,000,000 ESOL** under the current token configuration.

The **Update Authority** remains active for token metadata. This allows project metadata such as website and social links to be maintained without changing the token supply or transfer rules.
