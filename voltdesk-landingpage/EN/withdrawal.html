Withdrawal instruction

Consumers generally have a statutory right of withdrawal for contracts concluded at a distance, subject to the statutory exceptions and requirements applicable to digital products and services.

The specific withdrawal information applicable to a paid VoltDesk subscription is provided during the ordering process and in the legally required withdrawal notice. Please rely on the version supplied with your individual order.

Where a statutory right of withdrawal applies, the withdrawal period is generally fourteen days. The exact start and any statutory exceptions depend on the type of contract and the circumstances of the individual order.
