Key questions about VoltDesk, its purpose, paper trading, pricing and the role of recommendations.

What is VoltDesk?

VoltDesk is a trading-intelligence and paper-trading application designed to combine market data, analysis, recommendations and trading governance in one workflow.

Is VoltDesk a broker?

No. VoltDesk is not a bank, broker or execution venue. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, trading activity in VoltDesk is simulated.

Is VoltDesk investment advice?

No. VoltDesk provides analytical information and structured decision support. The user remains responsible for evaluating information and making any real trading decision.

Can I use VoltDesk for free?

VoltDesk can provide a demo and a free usage tier. The exact available functions and limits are shown in the current product and trial flow.

What does Pro cost?

The current advertised Pro price is €9.90 per month. If an annual price is offered, the checkout page is authoritative for the current terms.

What is the 14-day trial?

The trial is designed to provide access to the Pro feature set for 14 days so users can evaluate the complete workflow before deciding whether to continue.

Does VoltDesk predict markets?

No. VoltDesk is designed to structure information and support decisions. No tool can guarantee market outcomes.
