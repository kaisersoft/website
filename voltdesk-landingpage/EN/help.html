HELP & WORKFLOW

Understand the VoltDesk workflow.

VoltDesk is built around a simple sequence: discover, analyze, decide, govern and review. The exact features available depend on the current product tier.

CORE WORKFLOW

Five stages.

01

DISCOVER

Find instruments and setups using market discovery, watchlists and rankings.

02

CONTEXT

Understand price, volume, levels, volatility and relevant events.

03

DECIDE

Document a structured trade idea and its assumptions.

04

GOVERN

Apply risk limits, stops, position sizing and discipline mechanisms.

Paper Trading

Paper trading simulates trading decisions without placing real market orders. Results can differ materially from live trading.

Risk Engine

Risk functions are designed to make exposure, position sizing and protective rules visible before a simulated trade.

Analytics

Performance and trading data can be reviewed to identify patterns and improve the process.

News & Events

News and market events provide additional context. External data can be delayed, incomplete or incorrect.

IMPORTANT

VoltDesk supports decisions — it does not make them for you.

Always verify market information independently and consider your own financial situation, knowledge, experience and risk limits before making real trades.
