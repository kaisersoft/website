HOW IT WORKS

From market data to a structured trading process.

VoltDesk combines discovery, analysis, decision support and trading governance in a single workflow. The goal is not to predict the market, but to make the decision process more transparent and disciplined.

01 · Scan

Identify relevant instruments, market movements, volatility and potential setups through watchlists, ranking and market discovery.

02 · Analyze

Evaluate price action with technical context such as VWAP, volume, levels, patterns and multiple timeframes.

03 · Decide

Bring observations together into a structured trade context instead of relying on isolated charts or signals.

04 · Govern

Keep position size, risk limits, stops, exposure and trading rules visible before and during paper trades.

THE PRINCIPLE

Decision support, not market prediction.

VoltDesk is designed to support a trader's own decision-making. Recommendations and analytics are informational tools; the user remains responsible for any real trading decision.
