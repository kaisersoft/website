Why do I lose money with breakout setups?
AI JOURNAL ANALYST · EXAMPLE INSIGHT

I found a pattern across 147 example trades. Your breakout entries taken later in the session show a lower win rate than your earlier entries. The bigger difference is what happens around them: after a loss, your average position size increases and your entry criteria become less selective. The setup is not the whole story — the sequence around the trade matters.
